Sentence embeddings were generated using three explored models: averaging the word vectors of the words in a sentence, paragraph vectors, and specific word vectors [19]. The first model, AV GWV EC, averages the vectors in the word sequence w = < w1, x2, ...wn > [19]. After obtaining word vectors via BSWE, the same scheme was used to average the vectors in one sentence as in AV GWV EC [7].
