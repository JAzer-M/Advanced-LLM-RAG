The paper treats headers (column headings) as one of the core aspects of a table: a table is defined as a triple consisting of headers, cells, and caption, where each header indicates the property of a column [6]. The methods match the query against each aspect of the table, including headers [8]. At the same time, headers are not the only aspect used—the approach also matches against cells and caption [8], and the paper reports that caption was the most effective single aspect on their dataset [9].
