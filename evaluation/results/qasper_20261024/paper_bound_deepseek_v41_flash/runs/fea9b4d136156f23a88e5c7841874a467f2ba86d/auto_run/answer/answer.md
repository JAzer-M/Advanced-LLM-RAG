The paper determines that the decoder handles an easier task than the encoder through two comparisons of training effort: (1) adding more layers to the encoder achieves larger improvements than adding more layers to the decoder [1]; and (2) when fixing a well-trained decoder and updating only the encoder (and vice versa), the decoder converges faster than the encoder [1][5]. They interpret more required training time as indicating a more difficult task, so the decoder's faster convergence indicates an easier task [5]. These two results together suggest the decoder handles an easier task than the encoder in NMT [1], a conclusion also stated in the paper's summary and conclusions [2][3][4].
