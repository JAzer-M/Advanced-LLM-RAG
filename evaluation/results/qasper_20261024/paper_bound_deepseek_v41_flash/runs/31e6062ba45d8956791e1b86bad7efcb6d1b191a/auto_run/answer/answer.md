The paper explores three models for obtaining sentence vectors from word embeddings: averaging the vectors of the words in one sentence, paragraph vectors, and specific word vectors [5]. Its main model, AVGWVEC, averages the word-sequence vectors learned by the classical word2vec algorithm [5]. Word embeddings themselves are learned with the word2vec tool, mapping each word into a k-dimensional vector via a neural probabilistic language model [4]. The first model was also trained on different corpora to test domain impact [6], and training specific argumentation word embeddings (e.g., the BSWE model for BAS detection) improved performance [9].
