The paper explores three models for obtaining sentence vectors: averaging the vectors of the words in one sentence, paragraph vectors, and specific word vectors [3]. In the first model (AVGWVEC), the word embedding matrix is learned by the classical word2vec algorithm and the vectors in the word sequence are averaged [3]. The second model (PARAVEC) trains paragraph vectors using the distributed memory model of paragraph vectors (PV-DM), an extension of word2vec in which h is constructed from W and D, where W is the word vector matrix and D holds the paragraph vectors, mapping each paragraph to a unique vector [11]. The third model trains specific argumentation word embeddings (BSWE) for a certain category (BAS), and after obtaining the word vectors via BSWE, the same averaging scheme as in AVGWVEC is used [8].
