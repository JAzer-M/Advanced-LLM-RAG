The paper incorporates expert knowledge by letting the user specify a set of "anchor" words (relevance variables), so that latent factors are required to be informative about both correlations in the data and the expert-specified variables [7]. This anchored approach combines the information bottleneck with Correlation Explanation (CorEx), enabling users to leverage domain knowledge to guide exploration of documents and impose semantics onto the latent factors learned by CorEx [1]. Like seeded LDA, this allows the specification of word–topic relationships [6].
