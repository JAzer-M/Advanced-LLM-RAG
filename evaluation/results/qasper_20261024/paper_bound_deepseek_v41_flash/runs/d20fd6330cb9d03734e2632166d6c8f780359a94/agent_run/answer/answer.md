The improvement margin varies by comparison: ZAT shows a statistically significant average improvement of 2.58, 2.44 and 2.5 points over CT (another zero-shot model) with training sizes 500, 1000 and 2000, respectively [2], and an average improvement of 5.07 over CT when no target domain data is available [1]. Over LSTM-BoE, ZAT has a statistically significant mean improvement of 4.04, 5.37 and 3.27 points with training size 500, 1000 and 2000, respectively [3].
