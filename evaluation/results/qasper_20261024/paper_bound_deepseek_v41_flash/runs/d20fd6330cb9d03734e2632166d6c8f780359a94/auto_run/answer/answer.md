The paper reports the improvement margin in several comparisons. ZAT shows an average improvement of 5.07 over CT in the zero-shot setting where no target domain data is available [1]. ZAT also shows a statistically significant average improvement of 2.58, 2.44 and 2.5 points over CT with training size 500, 1000 and 2000, respectively [2]. Over LSTM-BoE, ZAT has a statistically significant mean improvement of 4.04, 5.37 and 3.27 points with training size 500, 1000 and 2000, respectively [3]. The model is also described as outperforming previous state-of-the-art systems by a large margin, with even higher improvement in the low data regime [6].
