The paper considers translation from English into four target languages: French, Italian, Spanish and German, with experiments on 3-class polarity classification in tweets [3]. The proposed approach transfers lexicon words through word embeddings aligned across languages with a simple linear transform, replacing machine translation [2]. The authors note the approach was successful for languages close to English where word-to-word translations are possible, but may be less effective for languages where this assumption does not hold [1].
