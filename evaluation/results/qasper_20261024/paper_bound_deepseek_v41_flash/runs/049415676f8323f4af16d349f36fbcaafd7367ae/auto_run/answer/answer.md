The paper states that using both pseudo labels and negative feedback yields significant improvements in precision and recall, but it does not report a single overall improvement figure (e.g., an absolute or relative percentage gain over the baseline) in the available passages [13]. It also reports that recall increases with self-distillation, giving the best F1 score [1], and that each of utilizing negative feedback and then additional pseudo labels shows statistically significant improvements with the McNemar test at p=0.05 for the final reranker results [1]. However, the specific magnitude of improvement on domain classification is not stated in these passages, so the evidence is insufficient to quantify "by how much" beyond the reported significant precision/recall gains [13] and significant reranker improvements [1].
