# p2: DPR 使用什么函数计算问题向量和段落向量的相似度？

## 回答

DPR 使用点积（dot product）计算问题向量与段落向量的相似度，即 sim(q, p) = EQ(q)ᵀEP(p) [1]。此外，论文也实验了其他相似度函数与损失组合，包括欧氏距离（L2）和 triplet loss [2]。

At run-time, DPR applies a different encoder EQ(·) that maps the input question to a d-dimensional vector, and retrieves k passages of which vectors are the closest to the question vector. We deﬁne the similarity between the question and the passage using the dot product of their vectors: sim(q, p) = EQ(q)⊺EP (p).
