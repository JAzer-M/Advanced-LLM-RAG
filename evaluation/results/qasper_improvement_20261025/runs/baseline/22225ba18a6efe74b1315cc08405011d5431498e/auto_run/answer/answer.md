They trained a word2vec word embedding model on 189,206 externally downloaded financial articles (161,877,425 tokens) obtained from Factiva, from sources including the Financial Times and relating to US companies only [20]. This domain-specific external data was deliberately used because "the financial domain can contain very different language" [21]. These pre-trained embeddings were then used to create the positive and negative word groups (lists of the N most cosine-similar words to 'excellent' and 'poor') employed as features in their SVR system [12], [19].
