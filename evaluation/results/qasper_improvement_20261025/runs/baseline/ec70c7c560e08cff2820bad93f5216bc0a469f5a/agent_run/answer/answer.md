They experimented under three evaluation settings: IN-DOMAIN, OUT-OF-DOMAIN, and CROSS-DATASET, which allow evaluating both the quality of domain-aware text representation and adaptation to unfamiliar domains [1]. Experiments were performed mainly on their multi-domain MULTI-SUM dataset [6]. Source domains are defined as the first five domains in Table 1 (IN-DOMAIN), while the other domains (OUT-OF-DOMAIN) are totally invisible during training; IN-DOMAIN tests learning different domain distributions on a multi-domain set, and OUT-OF-DOMAIN tests performance on unseen domains [6]. They investigated four learning strategies for dealing with domain shift under these three settings [2][1], and additionally designed a simple analytical experiment on the MULTI-SUM dataset where they take turns choosing one domain and train the basic model on its training data to verify the existence of domain shift [3].
