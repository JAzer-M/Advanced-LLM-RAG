Troll labels are obtained in two ways depending on the scenario. In the fully supervised setting, the troll roles are manually assigned labels (Left Troll, Right Troll, News Feed, Commercial, Fearmonger, Hashtag Gamer, Non English, Unknown) from the dataset collected and published by Linvill and Warren (2018) [6]. In the distant supervision scenario, troll role labels are not available at training time, so labels for news media are used as a proxy, from which labels for the troll users are inferred [3]. These media labels are propagated to troll users that mention them via the mapping LEFT→left, RIGHT→right, CENTER→news feed, either by training a proxy model on media and applying it to users, or by using label propagation for semi-supervised learning [1]. By combining the media labels with the citation graph from troll accounts to news media, the final labeling of accounts can be inferred without any manual labeling [2].
