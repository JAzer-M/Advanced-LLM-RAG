Patterns correlated with emotional (FEELING) arguments included:

- Expressions with adjectives and active voice verb phrases [3].
- NP Prep patterns containing the preposition "for," which were substantially more common in the FEEL class; "for" was commonly used to express advocacy (e.g., *demand for*) or to refer to affected population groups (e.g., *treatment for*) [6].
- Patterns often based on the speaker's own beliefs or claims (perhaps assuming they themselves are credible), or involving assessment or evaluations of the arguments [4].

These patterns were extracted from annotated FACTUAL and FEELING debate forum posts and expanded via bootstrapping on unannotated posts [2].
