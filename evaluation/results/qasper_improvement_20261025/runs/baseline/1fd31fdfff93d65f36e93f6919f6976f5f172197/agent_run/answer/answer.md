The factual and feeling forum posts—quote-response pairs in the IAC corpus—were manually annotated with respect to whether each response is primarily a FACTUAL or FEELING based argument [10]. The annotation was done via a Mechanical Turk survey asking: "Is the respondent attempting to make a fact based argument or appealing to feelings and emotions?" [5]. Fact vs. Feeling was measured as a scalar ranging from -5 to +5, because previous work suggested that taking the means of scalar annotations reduces noise in Mechanical Turk annotations (Snow et al., 2008), and each pair was annotated by 5-7 annotators [9].
