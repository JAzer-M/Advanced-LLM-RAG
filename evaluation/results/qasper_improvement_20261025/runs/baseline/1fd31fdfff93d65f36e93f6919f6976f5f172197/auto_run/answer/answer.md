The forum posts were annotated via a Mechanical Turk survey presenting a slider scale from -5 to 5, with the question: "Is the respondent attempting to make a fact based argument or appealing to feelings and emotions?" [5] For the experiments, only the response texts were used, and each response was assigned a binary label: texts with score > 1 were assigned to the FACT class, and texts with score < -1 were assigned to the FEELING class [12]. Responses with scores between -1 and 1 were discarded because they had a very weak Fact/Feeling assessment, which could be attributed to responses containing aspects of both factual and feeling expression, or neither [12].
