The paper explores **news publications (i.e., data sources) as domains**. The authors extend the conventional definition of a domain from content categories to data sources for text summarization [10], and demonstrate that news publications can cause data distribution differences, meaning they can also be defined as domains [3]. The paper explores publication in the context of the domain and investigates the domain shift problem in summarization, proposing a multi-domain testbed for training and measuring performance on a set of domains [1].
