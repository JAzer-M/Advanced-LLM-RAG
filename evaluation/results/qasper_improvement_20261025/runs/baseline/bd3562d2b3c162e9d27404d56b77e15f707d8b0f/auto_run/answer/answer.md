The paper evaluates its systems using three metrics [3][5][6]:

- **Metric 1**: weighted cosine similarity, the original metric used to evaluate the final version of the results, where they were ranked 5th [3][5].
- **Metric 2**: equation 1, a classification metric for sentences with one aspect, which penalises predicted values of opposite sign (giving -1 score) and rewards values with the same sign (giving +1 score); the first version of the results were based on it, where they were ranked 4th [2][5].
- **Metric 3**: the organisers' equation as presented in Cortis et al. (2017); the second version of the results were based on it, where they were ranked 5th [5].
