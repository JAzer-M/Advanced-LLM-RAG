The speech (audio-text) alignment quality was evaluated through a manual human evaluation. Two annotators each rated 30 items from each bin, with 10 shared items to compute inter-annotator reliability [1]. The audio-text alignment was rated on a 3-point scale: 1 (wrong alignment), 2 (partial alignment, some words or sentences may be missing), 3 (correct alignment, allowing non-spoken syllables at start or end) [1]. Ratings were binned by hunalign confidence (Low/Moderate/High) and averaged over 90 items and two raters, with an average audio-text alignment score of 0.61 out of 3 [7]. Inter-annotator reliability for audio-text alignment ratings was measured with Krippendorff's α and scored 1.00 [8], and the alignment was rated as generally high quality [9].
