The four learning strategies investigated are:

1. **ModelI Base** – a simple but effective model for multi-domain learning, in which all domains are aggregated together and used for training a set of shared parameters [20].
2. **ModelII BERT** [12]
3. **ModelIII Tag** [12]
4. **ModelIV Meta** [12]

These four strategies take different angles to address the multi-domain learning task and the adaptation to new domains [12], and their effectiveness is examined under three evaluation settings: DOMAIN, OUT-OF-DOMAIN and CROSS-DATASET [2].
