The sentence alignment quality is evaluated manually. Two annotators each rated 30 items from each bin, with 10 items shared between them to calculate inter-annotator reliability [2].

The text (sentence) alignment quality is rated on a 5-point scale used in Kocabiyikoglu et al. (2018), ranging from "wrong alignment" (1) up to "correct alignment and fully compositional translation" (5), with intermediate levels covering partial alignments with compositional translation and varying amounts of additional or missing information [2][5].

The evaluation items are divided into bins based on the hunalign confidence score: Low (−0.3 < x ≤ 0.3), Moderate (0.3 < x ≤ 0.8), and High (0.8 < x), so the manual ratings reflect alignment quality across different automatic confidence ranges [10][2].
