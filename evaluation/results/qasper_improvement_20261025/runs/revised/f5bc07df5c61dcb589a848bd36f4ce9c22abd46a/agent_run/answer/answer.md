Other than privacy, the paper highlights the social impact and biases emanating from clinical notes and their processing as major ethical challenges in clinical data [1].

The paper's stated aim is to gather the ethical challenges especially relevant for clinical NLP; while enhancing privacy through restricted data access has been the norm, the authors state they "do not only discuss the right to privacy, but also draw attention to the social impact and biases emanating from clinical notes and their processing" [1].
