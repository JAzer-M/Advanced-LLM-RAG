In more detail, the paper observed that patterns associated with factual arguments often include topic-specific terminology, explanatory language, and argument phrases [3]. Syntactically, patterns with prepositional phrases (especially NP Prep) and passive voice verb phrases were more common for FACTUAL arguments; NP Prep instantiations are illustrated by FC1, FC5, FC8, FC10 and PassVP by FC2 and FC4 in Table 2 [4]. Additionally, learned NP Prep patterns with the preposition "of" were common in the FACT class, with "of" often attaching to objective terminology [7]. These patterns were extracted using a bootstrapping methodology from annotated FACTUAL and FEELING debate forum posts, extended to unannotated posts [2][9].
