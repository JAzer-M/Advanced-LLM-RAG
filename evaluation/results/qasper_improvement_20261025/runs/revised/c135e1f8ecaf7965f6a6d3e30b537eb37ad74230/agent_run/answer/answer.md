Labels for trolls are obtained in two ways depending on the scenario: via manual labeling in the supervised setting, and by propagating labels of news outlets mentioned by the trolls in the distant-supervision setting.

In the supervised scenario, each troll user is an example whose label is available for training thanks to manual labeling [4]; here ground-truth labels for troll users are available [1][2]. In the distant supervision scenario, labels for trolls are not available [2][3], so the paper relies on more commonly available labels for news outlets mentioned by the trolls [2][3], combined with a citation graph from troll accounts to news media to infer the labels on the accounts themselves without manual labeling [8]. Labels are propagated from the media to the troll user that mentions them via the mapping LEFT→left, RIGHT→right, CENTER→news feed [7], either by training a proxy model for media and applying it to users or by using label propagation for semi-supervised learning [7].
