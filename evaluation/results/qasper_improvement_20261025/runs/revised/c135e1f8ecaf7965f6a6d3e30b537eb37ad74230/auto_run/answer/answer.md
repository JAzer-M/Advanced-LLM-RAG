Labels for trolls are obtained either through manual labeling in the supervised scenario (T1), or, when labels are not available, they are inferred via distant supervision (T2) using labels of news outlets mentioned by the trolls.

In the fully supervised task, each troll user is an example whose label is available for training thanks to manual labeling [4]. In the more realistic distant-supervision scenario, troll labels are not available, so labels for news media are used as a proxy; labels are propagated from the media to troll users who mention them (LEFT→left, RIGHT→right, CENTER→news feed), e.g., by training a proxy model for media and applying it to users or via label propagation [3][6][7]. By combining media labels with the citation graph from trolls to news media, the labeling of the accounts can be inferred without manual labeling [8].
