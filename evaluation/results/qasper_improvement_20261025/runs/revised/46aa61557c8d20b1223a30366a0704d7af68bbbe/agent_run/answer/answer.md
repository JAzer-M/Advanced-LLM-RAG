The sentence (text-to-text) alignment quality is evaluated through a manual evaluation using a 5-point scale, with items binned by hunalign confidence score and inter-annotator reliability measured by Krippendorff's α.

Specifically, the text alignment quality was evaluated according to the 5-point scale used in Kocabiyikoglu et al. (2018), ranging from 1 (wrong alignment) to 5 (correct alignment and fully compositional translation) [3][4]. The evaluation was performed by two annotators, each rating 30 items per bin, with 10 overlapping items between annotators used to compute inter-annotator reliability [4]. Items were grouped into bins (Low, Moderate, High) according to the hunalign confidence score [8]. Inter-annotator agreement was measured with Krippendorff's α for ordinal ratings, scoring 0.77 for text-to-text alignment quality ratings [7].
