The paper explores domains defined as news publications/data sources rather than topic categories, focusing on extractive summarization and demonstrating that news publications can be treated as domains [1][4][6][10].

The paper extends the conventional definition of domain from categories into data sources for summarization [1], and specifically demonstrates that news publications can cause data distribution differences and thus can be defined as domains [6][10]. The experiments involve CNN/Daily Mail and a multi-domain testbed referred to as MULTI-SUM domains [5], where the top five publications are used as source domains for training and the bottom ones viewed as OUT-OF-DOMAIN [3].

However, the complete list of the specific news publications making up the multi-domain testbed (e.g., which publications are included in MULTI-SUM) is not stated in the supplied passages, so this gap remains unanswered.
