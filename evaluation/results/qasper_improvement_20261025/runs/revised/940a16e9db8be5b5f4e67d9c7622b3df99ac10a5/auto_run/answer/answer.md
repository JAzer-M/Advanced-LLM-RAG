The paper explores domains defined by news publications (data sources) for extractive text summarization, extending the concept of domain from conventional categories into article sources, with the top five publications used as source domains for training and the bottom ones viewed as OUT-OF-DOMAIN.

The authors extend the conventional definition of the domain from categories into data sources for the text summarization task [4], focusing on extractive summarization and demonstrating that news publications can cause data distribution differences, meaning they can also be defined as domains [10]. Publications are used as domains because they can be easily obtained and clearly defined [6]. The top five publications are used as source domains for training and the bottom ones are viewed as OUT-OF-DOMAIN [3].
