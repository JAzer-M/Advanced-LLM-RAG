The speech (audio-text) alignment quality is evaluated through a manual human evaluation using a 3-point rating scale [1].

The corpus was split into three bins according to ranges (−0.3, 0.3], (0.3, 0.8] and (0.8, ∞) of the hunalign confidence score [9]. Two annotators each rated 30 items from each bin, with 10 items shared between them to compute inter-annotator reliability [1]. The audio-text alignment quality was rated on a 3-point scale: 1 = wrong alignment, 2 = partial alignment (some words or sentences may be missing), 3 = correct alignment, allowing non-spoken syllables at start or end [1]. Inter-annotator agreement was measured with Krippendorff's α for ordinal ratings, scoring 1.00 for audio-text alignment ratings [7]. The audio-text alignment was rated as in general high quality [10].
