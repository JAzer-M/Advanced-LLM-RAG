No, the authors did not collect new data for evaluation; both datasets they used were pre-existing resources created by other authors.

The first dataset, the Czech Facebook dataset, was created by collecting posts from popular Facebook pages in Czech by Habernal et al. (2013) [20], and the second dataset they use contains user reviews about household devices purchased at mall.cz from Veselovská et al. (2012) [22]. Since both evaluation datasets were created by prior work, the authors of the survey collected no new data themselves.
