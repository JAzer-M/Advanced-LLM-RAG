The paper studies both Chinese and English.

The Chinese side comes from the E-commerce data, whose word vectors are taken from the Chinese-Word-Vectors resource [4]. The English side comes from the Quora dataset, as shown by English example questions such as "what is the capital in <location>?" discussed alongside the E-commerce example [10]. The Quora dataset is the original Quora question pairs dataset for the duplicated question detection task [13][16]. No other languages are indicated in the passages.
