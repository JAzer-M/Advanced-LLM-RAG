The paper reports that its methodology improves over the state of the art in the supervised scenario, and provides a compelling case for the distant-supervision scenario, which had not been explored before. In this context, "state-of-the-art" refers to the existing best-performing methods for predicting the role of political trolls, against which the paper's approach shows improvements on the "IRA Russian Troll" dataset (pages 1, 9).
