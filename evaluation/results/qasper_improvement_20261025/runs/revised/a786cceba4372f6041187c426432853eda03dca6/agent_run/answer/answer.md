According to the paper, its methodology improves over the state of the art in the traditional, fully supervised scenario, while the distant-supervision scenario had no prior state of the art, as it had not been explored in the literature before.

Experiments on the "IRA Russian Troll" dataset show the paper's methodology improves over the state-of-the-art in the supervised scenario [1][2]. The paper also states it improves over the state of the art in the traditional, fully supervised setting, where training labels are available [6]. For the second scenario, it proposes a novel distant supervision approach based on graph embeddings, BERT, and label propagation, projecting labels for news media onto the trolls who cited these media; the paper states this scenario "has not been explored in the literature thus far," so no prior state-of-the-art existed there [2][6].
