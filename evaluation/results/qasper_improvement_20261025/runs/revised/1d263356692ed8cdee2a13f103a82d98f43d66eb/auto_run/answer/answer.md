Chinese classical poetry is classified into two primary categories, SHI and CI [5]. The framework generates two major forms of SHI, Jueju and Lvshi, as well as 121 major forms (Cipai) of CI using a single model [1]. CI is another primary type of Chinese poetry, with nearly one thousand forms, each (a Cipai) defined by a fixed number of lines and a fixed number of characters per line [2].
