The paper uses two metrics: **coverage** and **precision**.

Coverage is defined as n_cover / n_all, where n_cover is the number of test-data phrases for which the solution finds corresponding patterns and n_all is the total number of phrases; precision is n_correct / n_cover, where n_correct is the number of phrases whose assigned patterns are correct [2]. Precision was evaluated by randomly selecting 100 verb phrases from the test data and having volunteers label whether the assigned patterns were correct [2].
